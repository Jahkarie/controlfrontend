# On D' Road: guest site (`controlfrontend`)

On D' Road is an invite-only, 18+ Antigua Carnival band. This repo is the guest-facing site (`index.html`) and, in `tickets/`, the public ticket site (tickets.ondroad.xyz). The API, admin pages and door scanner are in the separate `control` repo, whose `CLAUDE.md` describes the backend, known issues and features not built yet.

## Stack

- One static page, `index.html`: HTML, CSS and plain JavaScript. No build step, no framework.
- The look is "Night Kinetic" with a concrete front page. Two worlds that are each other's inverse, joined by one black top bar:
  - **The front (logged out), "the wall":** raw concrete (`--wall`, `art/concrete-wall.webp`) with thin circuit traces and crosshair registration marks, and the black-bar wordmark: ON D' ROAD in a black slab with a skyline/soundwave silhouette cut through it, pixel fragments breaking off, a cyan and safety-orange slash, and two slices of the letters shifted (one slice-glitch as it lands). Under it the `[ 18+ | Antigua ]` bracket tag and the coordinates (17°07′N · 61°51′W). How it works and the FAQ continue on concrete; the footer is black with the wordmark inverted. Sign-in is the night's charcoal: a sheet that slides up over the wall on phones, the right half of a split on laptops.
  - **The account (logged in), "the night":** matte charcoal with grain (`art/grain.png`, `art/grain-k.png` on black), one sharp accent (safety orange) for primary actions and the apostrophe, and concrete utility panels (`.cp`, `art/concrete.webp`) with crosshairs and circuit corners for the order, the pass ticket, the invite card and dialogs. The event cover is the sliced "D'" monogram on black with speed streaks. Cyan, acid green and purple only live in the streaks and the wordmark's slash.
  - Concrete with a black slab outside, night with concrete slabs inside. The pass ticket's black head (ON D' ROAD, the orange slash and pixel fragments) is the black-bar wordmark again in small.
- Fonts from Google Fonts: Archivo (variable, `wdth` 62–125 and `wght` 100–900; display type is expanded to `font-stretch: 125%`, the wordmark heavy 900 "ON D'" with a thin 200 "ROAD") and Space Mono (labels, tags, codes, coordinates, prices). Numbers use tabular figures. If the fonts don't load, display type falls back to a local bold (`Display Narrow`/`Display Wide`) so the big words still fit; the bar wordmark's text is fitted with `textLength`, so it keeps its width either way.
- Images next to it: `og.png` (1200×630 link preview: the black-bar wordmark on concrete with circuit traces, crosshairs, "Antigua Carnival · Invite only", `[ 18+ | Antigua ]` and the coordinates; a 256-colour PNG, about 110 KB, kept under 300 KB for WhatsApp and sized so the wordmark still reads as a small thumbnail), `favicon.svg` (the sliced D' monogram: a heavy wide chalk D cut into bands, two pushed sideways, with the orange apostrophe, on a rounded black tile; paths only, no fonts, edges on the 16px grid so it stays crisp in a tab), and `favicon-32.png` (transparent corners) and `apple-touch-icon.png` (180×180, opaque black, full bleed) rendered from it. `art/` holds `concrete-wall.webp` (the front's wall, a 640px tile, about 37 KB), `concrete.webp` (the panels' concrete, 512px, about 4 KB) and `grain.png` / `grain-k.png` (128px matte grain for charcoal and black). An event poster can live here too (e.g. `art/poster.jpg`, set on the backend's `/admin/event`). `trailer/` holds the trailer, silent and 15 seconds long, as `intro-9x16` (720×1280, for portrait screens) and `intro-16x9` (1280×720, landscape), each as `.webm` (VP9), `.mp4` (H.264) and a `.jpg` still (about 2.3 MB per video), with the CASHCXDE ENT. watermark burned in: the lock screen and "Watch the trailer" play it. The `og:url` and `og:image` tags point at `https://ondroad.xyz/`.
- Inline SVG: the sprite at the top of `<body>` holds `#barmark` (the black-bar wordmark; its colours come in through `--bw-bar`, `--bw-ink`, `--bw-line`, `--bw-slash` and `--bw-blade`, and `.bw-inv` inverts it for the footer), `#xh` (the crosshair) and `#i-ticket`. The front's circuit board is an inline `<svg class="circuit">`. The panels' crosshairs, circuit corners and select chevrons are data-URI tokens in `:root` (`--xh-ink`, `--xh-lite`, `--circ-ink-tr|bl`, `--circ-lite-tr|bl`, `--chev-lite`, `--chev-ink`). The empty `#sp-*`, `#seal-path` and `#evp-seal-path` from the old look stay so every id from before still exists.
- **Site lock head script:** a tiny inline `<script>` in `<head>`, before the first paint, sets `html.locked` (only the trailer shows) unless `localStorage` `odr_site_open` (the last answer from `/api/site`) is `'1'` or a preview token is stored (`odr_preview`). A `?preview=<token>` link is stored in `odr_preview` and taken out of the address bar (`history.replaceState`, other parameters kept). Any storage error means locked. It leaves the token in `window.odrPreview` for the main script.
- External scripts: `qrcodejs` from cdnjs, for the entry pass, and PayPal's button script from `www.paypal.com`, loaded only when the guest's reserved order can be paid with PayPal.
- Calls the backend at `BACKEND_URL` (set near the top of the `<script>`, currently `https://control-1-0baa.onrender.com`). The backend's CORS list must include wherever this page is served (`ondroad.xyz`, `www.ondroad.xyz`, `FRONTEND_URL`).
- **Main script:** every backend call goes through `backend(path, init)`, which adds `x-preview: <token>` when there's a preview token and, on a `423` (the site was locked after the page loaded), shows the lock screen and leaves the caller waiting (no toast, no error). `authFetch()` uses it, and so do the logged-out calls (`/api/site`, `/api/login`, `/api/login/verify`, `/api/invite/*`, `/api/packages`). The guest UI starts through `start()` (invite link, login link or saved session): straight away when the head script guessed open, otherwise only once `/api/site` says the site is open (see Site lock below).
- **Layout-only script:** a second, small `<script>` after the main one. It adds `html.lay` (so without it the sign-in form simply sits under the wall) and handles:
  - the phone sign-in sheet: `#hero-cta` and the bar's Get in open it (`body.signin-open`); the scrim, `#signin-x`, Escape and any `#` link inside close it. While open `#signin` is `role="dialog"` with `aria-modal`, everything behind it (bar, wall, How it works, FAQ, footer) is `inert`, and focus returns to the opener. A message in `#login-error` (a used invite, a bad login link) opens it so it's seen; logging in, an invite link or widening to the laptop layout closes it. On a laptop Get in just focuses the email field (scrolling only if it's out of view);
  - the bar's button (`#nav-cta`): sign in when logged out, `#evp-pass` on the event page, back to the top otherwise;
  - the bar's state: `.nav.solid` once the page scrolls 24px (the event page's bar starts clear over the cover), and `body.scrolled` past 55% of a screen, which slides the bar in on the logged-out phone front;
  - scroll-spy for the laptop nav (`#nav-links a[data-spy]` get `aria-current`);
  - the pass's "Full screen" button (`#pass-expand`) clicks More > Show my pass QR (`#mi-qr`).
  It makes no API calls, reads no storage and touches no guest data. The main script doesn't depend on it.
- **Tickets button:** the bar has "Tickets" (`#nav-tix`, with the ticket icon, left of `#nav-cta`; the outline style even where Get in is orange; the icon drops below 390px wide), and the phone front has "[ Tickets ]" (`#wall-tix`) in the wall's top-right corner, since the bar waits until the wall has scrolled away there (hidden on laptops, once logged in and on the invite form). Both link to `https://tickets.ondroad.xyz/` and stay hidden until, once `/api/site` has said this site is open, `showTickets()` asks `/api/tickets/site` (once, with a plain `fetch` without `x-preview`) and it answers `locked: false`; the ticket site's own lock, an error or an older backend keep them hidden. While the main site is locked nothing shows anyway.

## Page flow

- **Site lock (`#lock`):** while the backend's site lock is on, the whole site is the trailer and nothing else. `#lock` is full screen and black, with the trailer looping (`muted playsinline autoplay loop`, WebM first, then MP4, `object-fit: cover`), `intro-9x16` on a portrait screen and `intro-16x9` on a landscape one, switching live when the screen turns; no visible text, links or controls (Low Power Mode's play button is hidden and shows the still instead). Screen readers get one visually hidden line, "On D' Road. Invite only. 18+. Opening soon." Under `prefers-reduced-motion` or Data Saver the matching `.jpg` still shows and no video loads. Under `html.locked` everything else in `<body>` is `display: none` and the main script makes it `inert`, so it's out of the accessibility tree. `/api/site` decides: `locked: true` keeps (or brings back) the lock and sets `odr_site_open = '0'`; `locked: false` lifts it (a 400 ms fade, none under reduced motion), sets `odr_site_open = '1'` and starts the guest UI; no `locked` field (an older backend) means open; no answer (network, 5xx) keeps the head script's choice. While locked nothing but `/api/site` is requested: it's asked again every minute (so the site opens by itself at the unlock time), and after a failed try sooner (3, 6, 12 … up to 30 seconds), since Render's free plan can take a minute to wake and the trailer is the loading screen meanwhile. A `423 SITE_LOCKED` from any call mid-session shows the lock (open dialogs close); if the site opens again later, the page reloads. **Preview:** the owner's link (`/?preview=<token>`, from the backend's `/admin/site`) shows the real site with a small fixed mono tag "[ Preview · site locked ]" (`#preview-tag`) and an Exit link (clears `odr_preview`, reloads into the lock). While previewing `odr_site_open` stays `'0'`. A token the backend no longer takes (the link was reset) just gets the locked answer, and the stored token is cleared.
- **The front and `login-view`:** the wall with the black-bar wordmark, "Your invite is your way in." and Get in (`#hero-cta`). On phones the wall is the whole first screen and the bar waits until it has scrolled away; Get in opens the sign-in sheet. On laptops the wall sits on the left (sticky) and `#signin` (the login form, or the invite form) on the right, with How it works, FAQ and Contact in the bar. Under the wall's call to action (on laptops, under the tags) is "[ Watch the trailer ]" (`#watch-trailer`): it opens `#trailer-dlg`, a full-screen `<dialog>` with the trailer for the screen's shape and its controls (it plays at once, except under reduced motion or Data Saver), and a Close button; Escape or Close stops the video and focus returns to the button. It only exists on the open, logged-out front (the front is hidden once logged in, and everything is under the lock while locked). Enter email → `/api/login` emails a one-time link. How it works (`#how`) and the FAQ follow on concrete; `body.authed` hides the front and How it works.
- **`?login=<code>`:** exchanged at `/api/login/verify` for a session token.
- **`accept-view` (`?invite=<token>`):** on phones the bar shows and the wall shrinks to a short brand band (just the wordmark and tags) over the form; on laptops the form takes the sign-in panel's place. It shows who invited them and their note: "<inviter> chose you as one of their two invites.", or, when `/api/invite/:token` sends `inviter_kind: 'PROMOTER'` (a promoter's invite; `inviter` is then their name), "<Name> invited you to On D' Road.". The form (`#accept-form`) asks for the matching email, "Full name (as on your ID)" (`#accept-name`, `autocomplete="name"`, max 80), date of birth as Day / Month / Year selects (`#accept-dob-d|m|y`, years from 18 to 100 years back, Antigua's date) and Instagram (`#accept-ig`, with an `@` in front). There is no 18+ box: the date of birth is the check. It sends `{ token, email, full_name, birth_date: 'YYYY-MM-DD', instagram, adult: true }` (`adult` keeps an older backend accepting) and logs them in. Light checks run first (empty fields, a date that doesn't exist, under 18); `NAME_INVALID`, `UNDER_18`, `INSTAGRAM_INVALID` and `PROFILE_REQUIRED` (or the answer's `field`) show at their field (`#accept-*-err`), anything else in `#accept-error`.
- **Account states (`approval` from `/api/user-status`):** `#portal-view[data-acct]` is `loading` until the guest row arrives, then `approved`, `pending` or `declined`. A missing `approval` (older backend) means approved. Pending and declined are calm full-screen states (on a laptop, pending puts the statement on the left and the request on the right). **Pending:** the header says "Request sent." and that we'll email them once approved; `#acct-state` shows who invited them (`invited_by_name`, else the email, and "On D' Road" for `CONTROL`; for a promoter's invite `invited_by` is `'PROMOTER'` and `invited_by_name` the promoter's name, so it reads "Invited by <promoter>") and their details, with "Edit my details". The event page, packages, order, RSVP, pass and invites are hidden, and the nav button reads "My account". **Declined:** "Not this time." with a kind line, and nothing else. A reserve answer of `NOT_APPROVED` reloads the account into its state.
- **Event page (`#ev-page`, approved guests, the top of the account):** the cover is `event.poster` (an `https:` link or a relative path like `art/poster.jpg`, the same rule as sponsor logos; one that fails to load falls back) or the sliced D' monogram with speed streaks (`#evp-art`), and a black `[ Private · Invite only · 18+ ]` label (`.evp-tape`). Then the title (`#evp-title`): the event's `name` from `/api/event` (the backend's current event, e.g. a launch fete before the carnival), set as text; when there's no name (an older backend) or it is "On D' Road" itself, the styled "On D' Road" wordmark stays (`renderTitle()` keeps the original markup and puts it back); `event.when` (`#evp-when`, "Date coming soon" when empty) and the status line (`#evp-status`): "You're going · <package>" (plus "· Free pass"), "Spot held · <package> · pay by <date>" or "Pick your package". Three action buttons (`.evp-actions`; on phones a sticky bottom bar within thumb reach, the menu opening upward): **My pass** (`#evp-pass`: scrolls to and focuses the pass `#pass-card`; reads "Get your pass" until paid and goes to the order or the first package on sale), **Contact** (`#evp-contact`: the `#contact-dlg` sheet with WhatsApp, Instagram and email from `/api/site`, only the ones set; hidden when none are) and **More** (`#evp-more`/`#evp-menu`: "Guide" when there's an event guide (below); "Show my pass QR" when paid opens `#qr-dlg`; "Cancel my spot", "Request a refund" / "Cancel my free pass" and "Withdraw refund request" click the order panel's `#order-cancel`, `#order-refund` and `#order-withdraw`; FAQ, Terms, Log out). The menu is a disclosure: `aria-expanded`, arrow keys, Escape or a tap outside closes it and focus returns to More; sheets are `<dialog>`s that return focus to their opener. "The event" rows: **About** (`event.info`, hidden when empty), **Host** (On D' Road, with Instagram and WhatsApp icon links) and **Location** ("Secret location, revealed once you've paid" until paid, then `event.location` or "Details coming soon"), directly above the picker / order panel; then the paid-only details (`#event-paid`) or the "more details unlock" note (`#event-locked`). The pass (`#pass-card`) is a concrete ticket: a black head bar, "Entry pass · Admit one", the QR on white, holder, package and reference, and "Full screen" (`#pass-expand`); until paid it waits greyed with a lock. Phones: one column (cover, title and status, details, picker or order, then the pass; once paid the pass moves up under the status line), above the bottom bar. Laptops: a fixed left rail (cover, title, status, actions, the pass) and a main column (the rows, picker or order, the account, FAQ and footer), with Event, Guide (when there's one), Your package, Account and FAQ in the bar and My pass as its button. `#qr-dlg` is the full-screen pass on phones and a centred card on laptops. The old `#event-card` (with `#event-when`, `#event-info`) stays in the DOM, hidden and still filled.
- **Account section (under the event page):** the header (`#portal-title`, `#portal-sub`) still says "You're in." (no order), "Spot held." (reserved, with the pay-by date) or "You're going." (paid, with package, size and the event's "when"); then the email (`#user-email`), the **Your details** row (`#my-details`: name, age and @instagram with Edit; once paid, a note that the name and date of birth are locked), Log out and, once the order is `PAID` (free pass included), the Attendance card (`#rsvp-card`: "Confirm attendance" asks first, then it's locked, with no undo button and a note to request a refund instead; `toggleRSVP()` only ever sends `CONFIRMED`) and "invite your people" (`#invite-card`, `invites_left` from `/api/user-status`: the backend's allowance for the current event; `/api/send-invite` answers `PAY_FIRST` before that). Paid, attendance and invites are all per event on the backend: after a new event starts, a guest who paid for the last one sees the picker again.
- **Profile (only when `'full_name' in` the guest row):** `askProfile()` is one form with name, date of birth (three selects) and Instagram, saved through `POST /api/profile`; `400` codes show at their field and `403 PROFILE_LOCKED` under the fields (and the guest row reloads). Once the guest has paid (`has_paid` or a `PAID` order), only Instagram can change, unless the name or date of birth is still empty. A `404` turns the profile UI off. Paid guests with details missing get the "Finish your profile" box (`#profile-nudge`) on the event page, since staff check the name against photo ID at pickup.
- **Package picker (`#pkg-card`, "Get your pass"):** a single-choice list of rows split by hairlines; the chosen one gets `.chosen`: an orange check in its mark (`.pkg-pick`) and an orange bar down its left edge. Each row: name and price on one line (`price_cents`, the current tier's price), tags (the tier name as `.tier`, "Costume rules" when `requires_compliance`), what's included, the sale line (`.tier-info`: "On sale until <day, date · time> (Antigua time)" with a green light when the tier has `ends_at`, "N left at this price", "Then …" from `next_tier`), "Paying cash? <pay_note>" when `/api/packages` sends a `pay_note`, and spots left. With a backend that sends `sales_closed`, cards on sale come first (the `role="radiogroup"`), and closed or sold-out ones go into one `<details class="pkg-fold">` ("N not available right now") with their usual "Sales closed" / "Sold out" cards, dimmed; when nothing is on sale the fold starts open under a short line. Without `sales_closed` every card is listed as before.
- **Codes (`#promo-box`):** "Have a code?" checks it with `/api/promo/check` and shows what the guest pays ("20% off. You pay …"; a tracking code, `kind: 'TRACK'`, is full price and reads "Code applied. You pay …", never a discount); the code goes to `/api/orders` as `promo_code` along with the `tier_id` and `price_cents` the card showed. A free code turns the button into "Claim my free pass" and the order comes back `PAID`. On a reserved order, "Have a code?" uses `/api/orders/promo` (hidden once a PayPal checkout has started). Codes: `PROMO_INVALID` (shown at the code), `PRICE_CHANGED` (reload packages and show the new price), `SOLD_OUT` (reload packages). The code UI only appears when the backend sends `sales_closed`, so an older backend never shows it. **Shared code links:** `?code=XYZ` (what a promoter shares) is read on load (3 to 24 letters, numbers or dashes, else ignored), taken out of the address bar (other parameters kept) and kept in `localStorage` `odr_code` so it survives the login link; the first time an approved guest picks a package with the code box available, the box opens with the code filled in and checked, and `odr_code` is cleared.
- **Order panel (a concrete panel):** the amount is the order's own (`amount_cents`); `#order-price-note` shows the tier and code ("Code SAVE20: XCD 50.00 off"; a tracking code, `promo_kind: 'TRACK'` from `/api/my-order`, or one with nothing off reads "Code KAI applied"); the size select is disabled when `size_locked`; "How to pay" shows the general instructions and the package's `pay_note`; `#order-pickup` shows when the package was collected. Free passes (`paid_via === 'COMP'`) get their own wording, and the refund button reads "Cancel my free pass".
- **Reserving:** the "I agree to the terms" box must be ticked (sent as `terms: true`), and packages with sizes need one picked from "Your size". Guests with any of `full_name` / `birth_date` / `instagram` missing fill in the profile form first ("Your details first"). `/api/orders` can still answer `PROFILE_REQUIRED` (the form opens and the order is retried, at most 3 tries in all) or `NOT_APPROVED`. An older backend may answer `AGE_REQUIRED` instead; then the page asks "Are you 18 or older?" with `ask()` and resends with `adult: true`. The free-code notes say the name and date of birth can't change once the free pass is claimed. The order panel lets the guest change the size (`/api/orders/size`).
- **Pay now (`#paypal-box`):** on a reserved order, `/api/paypal/checkout` says whether PayPal is on for this guest and the USD amount. PayPal's buttons call `/api/paypal/create-order` and, once the guest approves, `/api/paypal/capture`. Codes: `ALREADY_PAID` (an earlier payment was found; reload the order), `PENDING` (PayPal is still processing; show the message instead of buttons), `CHECK` (problem with an earlier payment; contact us), `DECLINED` (`actions.restart()` so the guest can pick another card). When PayPal shows, the cash instructions are labelled "Other ways to pay".
- **Footer, terms, FAQ and sponsors:** `/api/site` (public) is loaded on every visit. The footer shows the organizers' WhatsApp, Instagram and email when set, a "Terms" link that opens `#terms-dlg`, an "FAQ" link, and a "With" row of sponsor logos (`#sponsors`; only `https:` links or relative paths such as `sponsors/acme.png`, which need a `sponsors/` folder in this repo). The FAQ is the `#faq` section after the account area (on concrete while logged out), one `<details>` per `# ` question; it's hidden when the FAQ is empty. The footer shows the inverted black-bar wordmark while logged out and a big ON D' ROAD in type once logged in. Terms and FAQ text go through `renderRich()` (`# ` headings, `- ` bullets, all set as text). The login screen points people who don't get the email to the FAQ and the contact details.
- **The event guide (`#guide-card`, `#guide-dlg`):** for a paid guest whose `/api/event` has `guide` (control's guide.js: only a guest with a `PAID` order for the current event, a free pass included, and only once the organizers publish it). "Your guide" is a card on the event page right under the status line on phones (`order: 2`, above the pass) and at the top of the main column on laptops: the meeting place in heavy wide type, "Meet · Sat 7 Nov · 9:30 PM" with a "Sharp" tag, on the day a "Now · …" (or "Next · …") line, and Open your guide; "Saved on this phone" when it's the kept copy. More > Guide (`#mi-guide`, first in the menu) and the laptop nav's Guide (`#nl-guide`) open the same sheet: full screen on phones, a 760px panel on laptops, with Close and Escape returning focus. The view (`GUIDE.render()`, between the `guide:render:start`/`end` markers, with its CSS between `guide:css:start`/`end`) is word for word the same code as control's `GUIDE_VIEW_JS`/`GUIDE_VIEW_CSS`, which the admin preview runs: change one, copy it to the other. It shows a header ("[ Your guide ]", the event's name, "Updated …", "Saved on this phone", "For paid guests only…"), then numbered sections with mono labels, each hidden when empty: **Meeting point** (the place and the time big, Sharp, the day and "Antigua time", "Open in Google Maps" / "Open in Apple Maps" built from the pin (`https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=lat,lng`, `https://maps.apple.com/?ll=lat,lng&q=<place>`) or one button for a link that had no pin, "How to get there", the picture), **Route** (a vertical line down numbered stop markers, the start orange and the end solid chalk), **Schedule** (time order; a time more than 6 hours before the meet time counts as after midnight; on the day, Antigua time, from midnight to three hours after the last item, a Now / Next box and Now / Next marks, earlier items dimmed; redrawn every 30 seconds), **Parking** (text, map buttons, picture, "Use at your own risk: not an official car park.") and **Rules** (Do / Don't / Heads up, each an icon and label in `--acid` / `--red` / `--amber`, the Don't box dashed so it isn't colour alone). The watermark tile (`--wm`) sits faint behind it. Everything is `textContent`; links are https only, open in a new tab with `noopener`; pictures follow the poster's rule. **Offline:** the guide is kept on the phone as `odr_guide_<email>` (the guide and the event's name) whenever `/api/event` sends it; with no signal (a network error) the kept copy shows, marked "Saved on this phone". It's dropped when `/api/event` answers without `guide` (unpublished, not paid), when `/api/my-order` says the order isn't `PAID` (refunded, cancelled) and on Log out. **`?guide=1`** (the "Your guide is ready" email's button) is taken out of the address bar and kept as `odr_guide_open` (like `odr_code`), so it survives the login link: the guide opens by itself once it's here and the account has loaded, once; an account with no guide clears it. An older backend sends no `guide`: nothing shows.
- The session is kept in `localStorage` as `control_token` and `control_user_email`. Requests go through `authFetch()` with `Authorization: Bearer <token>` (and through `backend()`, which adds `x-preview`). The site lock keeps `odr_site_open` (`'1'` or `'0'`, the last answer from `/api/site`) and `odr_preview` (the owner's preview token); a shared code link keeps `odr_code` until it's used; the guide keeps `odr_guide_<email>` (the copy for offline) and `odr_guide_open` (from `?guide=1`, until it opens).
- **The live pass:** once paid, `/api/my-order` sends `qr_secret`, and the QR is `ONDROAD:<reference>:<code>`, the code worked out on the phone with WebCrypto every 30 seconds (`liveCode()`: HMAC-SHA256 of `String(floor(unix seconds / 30))` with the secret's bytes, base32, 8 characters; the backend's `control/liveqr.js` does the same). The door takes this step ± 2, so a screenshot stops working after about a minute and a half. The pass card and `#qr-dlg` get a ring that drains (`.qr-ring`), "Live · new code in Ns" (`.qr-live-left`), the note "Live pass: show it at the entrance. Screenshots won't work." and the live watermark (`.live-wm`: the `--wm-ink` tile drifting, the holder's name in capitals with a running Antigua clock and LIVE repeated diagonally, and a sheen sweeping across; under reduced motion no drift or sheen, the tile hops every few seconds). It's all `aria-hidden` and click-through; the QR itself stays clean. The pass is kept on the phone as `odr_pass_<email>` (reference, package, secret) so it still shows without signal, and removed once the order isn't paid. An older backend sends no secret: then the old still QR `ONDROAD:<reference>:<email>` shows, as before (the new door only accepts that while the organizers allow it). The door scanner in `control/door.js` depends on these formats.
- **Watermark:** "ON D' ROAD + CASHCXDE ENT." as an SVG data-URI tile in `:root` (`--wm` chalk at 5% for dark, `--wm-ink` ink at 6.5% for concrete; rotated about −20°), as `.wm-over` (aria-hidden, `pointer-events: none`) over the event cover and the lock's trailer, in the footer band, and live over the pass (above). ## Conventions

- Use `toast()` for notices and `ask()` for confirm/prompt dialogs, never `alert`/`confirm`/`prompt`. `ask()` fields take `value`, `autocomplete`, `type: 'select'` with `options`, a `prefix` (like `@`), `attrs`, and `group` (several inputs on one row under one label); `focus: 'first'` focuses the first field, and `onSubmit(values, ui)` keeps the dialog open on `false`, with `ui.error(field, text)` for inline messages.
- Set user data with `textContent`, not `innerHTML`.
- Colours, fonts and decoration are CSS variables in `:root`; reuse them.
  - Night: `--black` `#0a0a0b`, `--char` `#141416` (the page), `--char-2` `#1a1a1d` and `--char-3` `#232327` (raised: menus, sheets), `--text` (chalk `#ecebe6`), `--muted`, `--dim`, and hairlines `--line` / `--line-2`.
  - Concrete: `--concrete` `#c9c8c3` / `--concrete-2` `#e4e3df` with `--concrete-tex` (panels), `--wall` `#dfdeda` with `--wall-tex` (the front), and type on concrete `--ink` `#141416`, `--ink-2`, `--ink-line`.
  - Accents: `--orange` `#ff5a1f` (also `--accent`), `--cyan` `#22e1ff`, `--acid` `#b6ff3b` (also `--green`), `--purple` `#8b3dff`; messages `--red` and `--amber`. The main script's inline styles use `--red`, `--green` and `--muted`, so those three must keep working on dark and on concrete.
  - Fonts: `--wide` (display; set `font-stretch: 125%` with it), `--body`, `--mono`. Layout: `--gut` (side gutter, 16px on phones), `--top` (the bar's height, 56px / 64px), `--ease`. Texture: `--matte`, `--matte-k`, `--concrete-tex`, `--wall-tex`, plus the SVG tokens above.
- Light scopes: `.cp` (concrete panels and dialogs) and `.wall` (the front, How it works, and the FAQ while logged out) redefine `--text`, `--muted`, `--dim` and the hairlines to ink-on-concrete values, so the same components work on both; focus outlines turn ink there. Body text keeps AA contrast. Orange and cyan are for marks, buttons and large type only, never small text on concrete; text on orange is `--ink`.
- The details that keep it from looking cheap: square corners, hairline rules instead of boxed cards, mono labels and `[ bracket ]` tags (`.tagb`, `.brk`), few type sizes used decisively, and decoration placed on purpose (the slash, the circuit traces, the crosshairs), never everywhere.
- Decorative SVG and art is `aria-hidden="true"`: the sprite, the wall's circuit and crosshairs (`.hero-art`), the bar-wordmark SVGs (the front's `<h1>` carries the name as `.sr` text), the footer marks (`.foot-bw`, `.foot-mark`), the event cover's streaks and monogram (`.kin`, `#evp-art`), the ticket's slash and pixels, and the coordinates.
- Motion: the wordmark's one slice-glitch, the cover's streaks zipping in, `.reveal` fade-ins, the sheet sliding up. All of it respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Keep `:focus-visible` outlines.
- Phone below 1024px, laptop from 1024px (the layout script's `matchMedia` uses the same breakpoint). No sideways scrolling at 360 or 390px; tap targets are 44px or more.
- Guest-facing text is short, plain and on-brand ("Get in", "Bring your people").
