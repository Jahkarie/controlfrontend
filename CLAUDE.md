# On D' Road: guest site (`controlfrontend`)

On D' Road is an invite-only, 18+ Antigua Carnival band. This repo is the guest-facing site. The API, admin pages and door scanner are in the separate `control` repo, whose `CLAUDE.md` describes the backend, known issues and features not built yet.

## Stack

- One static page, `index.html`: HTML, CSS and plain JavaScript. No build step, no framework.
- The look is "Paint & Powder": a black canvas with neon paint splats (hot pink, yellow, cyan, lime), dripping condensed headlines, torn-tape labels, sticker buttons and a paper ticket for the entry pass.
- Fonts from Google Fonts: Archivo (variable; extra-condensed black for headlines, normal width for body), Space Mono (labels, codes) and Permanent Marker (graffiti tags like "Play mas!"). If they don't load, headlines fall back to a local narrow bold font (`Display Narrow`/`Display Wide`) so the big words still fit.
- Images next to it: `og.png` (1200×630 link preview: ON D' ROAD over a pink splat with drips and an "ANTIGUA CARNIVAL · 18+ · INVITE ONLY" tape label; a 256-colour PNG, kept under 300 KB for WhatsApp), `favicon.svg` (a pink splat with a chalk "D" and yellow apostrophe on black; paths only, no fonts), and `favicon-32.png` and `apple-touch-icon.png` (180×180, opaque black) rendered from the same mark. `art/powder-burst.webp` (AI-made neon powder cloud on black, about 64 KB) sits behind the hero and the event page's paint-art cover as the `.dust` layer, screen-blended and `aria-hidden`. An event poster can live here too (e.g. `art/poster.jpg`, set on the backend's `/admin/event`). The `og:url` and `og:image` tags point at `https://ondroad.xyz/`.
- External scripts: `qrcodejs` from cdnjs, for the entry pass, and PayPal's button script from `www.paypal.com`, loaded only when the guest's reserved order can be paid with PayPal.
- Calls the backend at `BACKEND_URL` (set near the top of the `<script>`, currently `https://control-1-0baa.onrender.com`). The backend's CORS list must include wherever this page is served (`ondroad.xyz`, `www.ondroad.xyz`, `FRONTEND_URL`).

## Page flow

- **`login-view`:** enter email → `/api/login` emails a one-time link.
- **`?login=<code>`:** exchanged at `/api/login/verify` for a session token.
- **`accept-view` (`?invite=<token>`):** shows who invited them and their note. The form (`#accept-form`) asks for the matching email, "Full name (as on your ID)" (`#accept-name`, `autocomplete="name"`, max 80), date of birth as Day / Month / Year selects (`#accept-dob-d|m|y`, years from 18 to 100 years back, Antigua's date) and Instagram (`#accept-ig`, with an `@` in front). There is no 18+ box: the date of birth is the check. It sends `{ token, email, full_name, birth_date: 'YYYY-MM-DD', instagram, adult: true }` (`adult` keeps an older backend accepting) and logs them in. Light checks run first (empty fields, a date that doesn't exist, under 18); `NAME_INVALID`, `UNDER_18`, `INSTAGRAM_INVALID` and `PROFILE_REQUIRED` (or the answer's `field`) show at their field (`#accept-*-err`), anything else in `#accept-error`.
- **Account states (`approval` from `/api/user-status`):** `#portal-view[data-acct]` is `loading` until the guest row arrives, then `approved`, `pending` or `declined`. A missing `approval` (older backend) means approved. **Pending:** the header says "Request sent." and that we'll email them once approved; `#acct-state` shows who invited them (`invited_by_name`, else the email, and "On D' Road" for `CONTROL`) and their details, with "Edit my details". The event page, packages, order, RSVP, pass and invites are hidden, and the nav button reads "My account". **Declined:** "Not this time." with a kind line, and nothing else. A reserve answer of `NOT_APPROVED` reloads the account into its state.
- **Event page (`#ev-page`, approved guests, the top of the account):** replaces the big header (`body.evp-on` hides `.hero` and the tape). Cover: `event.poster` (an `https:` link or a relative path like `art/poster.jpg`, the same rule as sponsor logos; one that fails to load falls back) or the paint art (`#evp-art`) with the powder burst, and a taped "Private · Invite only · 18+" label. Then "On D' Road" (`#evp-title`), `event.when` (`#evp-when`, "Date coming soon" when empty) and the status line (`#evp-status`): "You're going · <package>" (plus "· Free pass"), "Spot held · <package> · pay by <date>" or "Pick your package". Three sticker buttons: **My pass** (`#evp-pass`: scrolls to and focuses the pass `#pass-card`; reads "Get your pass" until paid and goes to the order or the first package on sale), **Contact** (`#evp-contact`: the `#contact-dlg` sheet with WhatsApp, Instagram and email from `/api/site`, only the ones set; hidden when none are) and **More** (`#evp-more`/`#evp-menu`: "Show my pass QR" when paid opens `#qr-dlg`; "Cancel my spot", "Request a refund" / "Cancel my free pass" and "Withdraw refund request" click the order panel's `#order-cancel`, `#order-refund` and `#order-withdraw`; FAQ, Terms, Log out). The menu is a disclosure: `aria-expanded`, arrow keys, Escape or a tap outside closes it and focus returns to More; sheets are `<dialog>`s that return focus to their opener. Rows: **Location** ("Secret location, revealed once you've paid" until paid, then `event.location` or "Details coming soon"), directly above the picker / order panel; **Host** (On D' Road, with Instagram and WhatsApp icon links); **About** (`event.info`); then the paid-only details (`#event-paid`) or the "more details unlock" note (`#event-locked`). Phones: one column (cover, details, picker or order, pass, host and about). Desktop: cover and pass on the left, the rest on the right. The old `#event-card` (with `#event-when`, `#event-info`) stays in the DOM, hidden and still filled.
- **Account section (under the event page):** the header (`#portal-title`, `#portal-sub`) still says "You're in." (no order), "Spot held." (reserved, with the pay-by date) or "You're going." (paid, with package, size and the event's "when"); then the email (`#user-email`), the **Your details** row (`#my-details`: name, age and @instagram with Edit; once paid, a note that the name and date of birth are locked), Log out, RSVP and, once the order is `PAID` (free pass included), "invite your people" (`#invite-card`, 2 invites; `/api/send-invite` answers `PAY_FIRST` before that).
- **Profile (only when `'full_name' in` the guest row):** `askProfile()` is one form with name, date of birth (three selects) and Instagram, saved through `POST /api/profile`; `400` codes show at their field and `403 PROFILE_LOCKED` under the fields (and the guest row reloads). Once the guest has paid (`has_paid` or a `PAID` order), only Instagram can change, unless the name or date of birth is still empty. A `404` turns the profile UI off. Paid guests with details missing get the "Finish your profile" box (`#profile-nudge`) on the event page, since staff check the name against photo ID at pickup.
- **Package picker (`#pkg-card`, "Get your pass"):** single choice; the chosen card gets `.chosen`, a check in its mark (`.pkg-pick`) and a pink border. Each card: name and price on one row (`price_cents`, the current tier's price), tags (the tier name as `.tier`, "Costume rules" when `requires_compliance`), what's included, the sale line (`.tier-info`: "On sale until <day, date · time> (Antigua time)" with a green light when the tier has `ends_at`, "N left at this price", "Then …" from `next_tier`), "Paying cash? <pay_note>" when `/api/packages` sends a `pay_note`, and spots left. With a backend that sends `sales_closed`, cards on sale come first (the `role="radiogroup"`), and closed or sold-out ones go into one `<details class="pkg-fold">` ("N not available right now") with their usual "Sales closed" / "Sold out" cards, dimmed; when nothing is on sale the fold starts open under a short line. Without `sales_closed` every card is listed as before.
- **Codes (`#promo-box`):** "Have a code?" checks it with `/api/promo/check` and shows what the guest pays; the code goes to `/api/orders` as `promo_code` along with the `tier_id` and `price_cents` the card showed. A free code turns the button into "Claim my free pass" and the order comes back `PAID`. On a reserved order, "Have a code?" uses `/api/orders/promo` (hidden once a PayPal checkout has started). Codes: `PROMO_INVALID` (shown at the code), `PRICE_CHANGED` (reload packages and show the new price), `SOLD_OUT` (reload packages). The code UI only appears when the backend sends `sales_closed`, so an older backend never shows it.
- **Order panel:** the amount is the order's own (`amount_cents`); `#order-price-note` shows the tier and code; the size select is disabled when `size_locked`; "How to pay" shows the general instructions and the package's `pay_note`; `#order-pickup` shows when the package was collected. Free passes (`paid_via === 'COMP'`) get their own wording, and the refund button reads "Cancel my free pass".
- **Reserving:** the "I agree to the terms" box must be ticked (sent as `terms: true`), and packages with sizes need one picked from "Your size". Guests with any of `full_name` / `birth_date` / `instagram` missing fill in the profile form first ("Your details first"). `/api/orders` can still answer `PROFILE_REQUIRED` (the form opens and the order is retried, at most 3 tries in all) or `NOT_APPROVED`. An older backend may answer `AGE_REQUIRED` instead; then the page asks "Are you 18 or older?" with `ask()` and resends with `adult: true`. The free-code notes say the name and date of birth can't change once the free pass is claimed. The order panel lets the guest change the size (`/api/orders/size`).
- **Pay now (`#paypal-box`):** on a reserved order, `/api/paypal/checkout` says whether PayPal is on for this guest and the USD amount. PayPal's buttons call `/api/paypal/create-order` and, once the guest approves, `/api/paypal/capture`. Codes: `ALREADY_PAID` (an earlier payment was found; reload the order), `PENDING` (PayPal is still processing; show the message instead of buttons), `CHECK` (problem with an earlier payment; contact us), `DECLINED` (`actions.restart()` so the guest can pick another card). When PayPal shows, the cash instructions are labelled "Other ways to pay".
- **Footer, terms, FAQ and sponsors:** `/api/site` (public) is loaded on every visit. The footer shows the organizers' WhatsApp, Instagram and email when set, a "Terms" link that opens `#terms-dlg`, an "FAQ" link, and a "With" row of sponsor logos (`#sponsors`; only `https:` links or relative paths such as `sponsors/acme.png`, which need a `sponsors/` folder in this repo). The FAQ is the `#faq` section after the account area, one `<details>` per `# ` question; it's hidden when the FAQ is empty. Terms and FAQ text go through `renderRich()` (`# ` headings, `- ` bullets, all set as text). The login screen points people who don't get the email to the FAQ and the contact details.
- The session is kept in `localStorage` as `control_token` and `control_user_email`. Requests go through `authFetch()` with `Authorization: Bearer <token>`.
- The QR code encodes `ONDROAD:<reference>:<email>`; the door scanner in `control/door.js` (entry and package pickup) depends on that format.
- The page must keep working with the previous backend, since this repo deploys first: treat missing fields as "feature off" and a 404 from a new endpoint as "not available".

## Conventions

- Use `toast()` for notices and `ask()` for confirm/prompt dialogs, never `alert`/`confirm`/`prompt`. `ask()` fields take `value`, `autocomplete`, `type: 'select'` with `options`, a `prefix` (like `@`), `attrs`, and `group` (several inputs on one row under one label); `focus: 'first'` focuses the first field, and `onSubmit(values, ui)` keeps the dialog open on `false`, with `ui.error(field, text)` for inline messages.
- Set user data with `textContent`, not `innerHTML`.
- Colors, fonts and paint shapes are CSS variables in `:root`; reuse them. Palette: `--bg` (night `#09080b`), `--surface` (card `#131117`), `--text` (chalk `#f6f1e8`), `--muted`, `--dim`, `--ink` (`#0b0a0d`, text on paint), `--pink` `#ff2e93`, `--yellow` `#ffe500`, `--cyan` `#1ee3ff`, `--lime` `#a3ff2b`, `--red` `#ff4d4d`; the script also uses `--accent`, `--green` and `--amber`. Fonts: `--display`, `--body`, `--mono`, `--tag`. Shapes: `--m-splat`/`--m-drip` (CSS masks), `--torn` (torn-tape clip-path) and the `#sp-*` splats in the SVG sprite at the top of `<body>`.
- Decorative SVG and art (splats, sprite, seal, "Play mas!" tags, the marquee tape) is `aria-hidden="true"`. Body text keeps AA contrast; text on paint is `--ink`.
- Animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Keep `:focus-visible` outlines. No sideways scrolling at 390px.
- Guest-facing text is short, plain and on-brand ("Get in", "Bring your people").
